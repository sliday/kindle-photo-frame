# Sample photos

Film photographs by the repository owner, used for the renders, the preview GIF and the library cover. They show no people, carry no metadata (no EXIF, GPS or camera data), and were run through `tools/prep-photos.sh` (1072 × 1448, grayscale, contrast stretch, light sharpening).

| File | Photograph |
|---|---|
| `bell-tower.jpg` | a bell tower between old houses |
| `snow-hut.jpg` | a hut in a snowy forest |
| `stairwell.jpg` | a curved stairwell under a window |

© the repository owner. They are here as samples for this project and are not covered by the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.

Your own photos go in `photos/kindle-frames/` (git-ignored) and onto the Kindle with `tools/install.sh`.
