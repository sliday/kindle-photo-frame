# Sample photos

Public-domain film photographs used for the renders, the preview GIF and the library cover. All three are US government works: Farm Security Administration and Office of War Information photographs in the Library of Congress, which lists them with "No known restrictions on publication". Each was run through `tools/prep-photos.sh` (1072 × 1448, grayscale, contrast stretch, light sharpening).

| File | Photograph | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lange-migrant-mother.jpg` | Dorothea Lange, *Destitute pea pickers in California. Mother of seven children* ("Migrant Mother"), Nipomo, California, March 1936, FSA; Library of Congress digital ID fsa.8b29516 | Public domain (US government work) | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lange-MigrantMother02.jpg) |
| `delano-victory-garden.jpg` | Jack Delano, railroad workers cultivating a Victory garden at Proviso yard, Chicago (Kodachrome, 1942-43), OWI; Library of Congress LCCN 2017878069 | Public domain (US government work) | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:C_%26_NW_RR_(i.e._Chicago_and_North_Western_railroad),_railroad_workers_cultivating_a_little_Victory_garden_at_Proviso_yard,_Chicago,_Ill._LCCN2017878069.tif) |
| `evans-allie-mae-burroughs.jpg` | Walker Evans, *Allie Mae Burroughs*, Hale County, Alabama, 1936, Resettlement Administration / FSA; Library of Congress digital ID fsa.8c52248 | Public domain (US government work) | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Allie_Mae_Burroughs,_8c52248u.tif) |
