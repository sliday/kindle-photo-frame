# Sample photos

Public-domain film photographs used for the renders, the preview GIF and the library cover. Each was run through `tools/prep-photos.sh` (1072 × 1448, grayscale, contrast stretch, light sharpening).

| `evans-havana.jpg` | Walker Evans, *Plaza del Vapor, Havana, Cuba* | Public domain | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plaza_del_Vapor_Havana,_Cuba_Walker_Evans.jpg) |
